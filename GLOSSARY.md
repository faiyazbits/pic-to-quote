# Glossary

**Shop Owner**: The person who runs the lighting shop and uses the app. They build quotes and send them out. Not "user", "admin" or "seller".

**Customer**: The shop's customer, who receives a Catalogue PDF and/or Quotation PDF. They never use the app. Not "client" or "buyer".

**Product**: One item the shop sells, such as a 2x2 ft square LED panel. It has a Product Code, a description, specs, a photo and a List Price.

**Product Code**: The short unique code the shop uses for a Product (e.g. `PNL-SQ-36`). It is one of the two ways to search the catalogue; the other is the description.

**Colour Temperature**: The light colour a Product is supplied in: Warm (3000K), Neutral (4000K) or Cool (6500K). It is chosen per Line Item, not per Product.

**Quote Draft**: The selection the Shop Owner is building for one Customer before sending. It holds Line Items, Customer details, an optional discount and a quote number. There is one Quote Draft at a time.

**Line Item**: One row of a Quote Draft: a Product in one Colour Temperature, with a quantity and a Quoted Price. The same Product in two Colour Temperatures is two Line Items.

**List Price**: The Product's standard price before GST.

**Quoted Price**: The unit price on a Line Item. It starts as the List Price and the Shop Owner may change it. Both PDFs always show the Quoted Price.

**Catalogue PDF**: A brochure of the Line Items in a Quote Draft: photo, specs, Colour Temperature and Quoted Price. No totals.

**Quotation PDF**: A formal, numbered price offer for a Quote Draft: Line Items with quantities, discount, GST, grand total, validity and terms.

**Shop Details**: The shop's own name, address, contact details, GSTIN, logo and standard terms. They are printed on every PDF.
